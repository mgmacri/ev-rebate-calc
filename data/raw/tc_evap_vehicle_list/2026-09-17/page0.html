Electric Vehicle Affordability Program: Vehicle list

This is a list of affordable electric vehicles with a manufacturer suggested retail price (MSRP) of $50,000 or less. This list can help Canadians who want to buy or lease an electric vehicle and potentially benefit from EVAP incentives.

Eligibility for the incentive is based on the final transaction value, which must not exceed $50,000.

It is important to note that if a vehicle is included on the vehicle list, this does not guarantee that every purchase or lease of that vehicle will quality for an incentive. Also, if a vehicle isn’t on the list, but meets the eligibility criteria and the final transaction value of $50,000 or less, then the transaction may qualify for the incentive.

Learn more on “final transaction value”.
